What to know

Old Persian cuneiform was used mainly for royal inscriptions from around the sixth to fourth centuries BCE. It is not the same writing system as modern Persian.

This tool maps the sounds of a modern name to the closest available signs. It is educational and decorative—not an authoritative historical translation.

For best results, spell the name the way it sounds. Common combinations such as sh, ch, kh, and gh are handled together.
